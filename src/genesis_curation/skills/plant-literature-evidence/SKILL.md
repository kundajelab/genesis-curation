# Originating papers and sample attribution

Separate originating articles, citing articles, reviews and repository links. Require
accession/data-availability evidence, then exact sample identifiers or unique informative
labels. Record inaccessible full text, failed retrieval and ambiguous sample mappings.
Mixed-study methods do not apply to every sample. Retain the source passage and mapping;
never manufacture paper attribution.
