# Study review and release

Group equivalent questions by study while preserving per-sample evidence. Explain old and
proposed values, source conflicts and missing evidence. Apply decisions only to explicitly
selected records and current versions. Do not propagate a majority label across biological
differences. Preserve human decisions, reasons and source/change history. Clean export
requires current human sign-off; unknown values and exclusions remain in the audit bundle.
